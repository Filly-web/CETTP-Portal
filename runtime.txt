# Fixed Asset Accounting & Control Policy Configuration

Before production posting, Finance must approve and configure:

- Capitalization threshold and low-value asset policy.
- Major/minor category hierarchy and GL cost, accumulated depreciation and depreciation expense mappings.
- Componentization rules for significant parts of buildings, vehicles and equipment.
- Useful-life and residual-value matrices by asset class.
- Depreciation convention (daily/monthly), placed-in-service start rule, disposal stop rule and financial-year start month.
- Reducing-balance annual rates and units-of-production usage capture.
- Impairment indicators, recoverable amount process and impairment approvals.
- Disposal approval limits, proceeds evidence and accounting journal mapping.
- CWIP project approval and capitalization criteria.
- Accounting versus tax depreciation rules, tax allowance classes, tax WDV and enacted deferred-tax rates.
- IAS 16 / IAS 38 disclosure policy and reporting period definitions.
- Reconciliation tolerances, materiality and resolution evidence requirements.
- User access matrix, maker-checker responsibilities and audit retention period.

The application does not infer accounting policy from asset descriptions. Finance must approve the policy parameters before live financial runs.
