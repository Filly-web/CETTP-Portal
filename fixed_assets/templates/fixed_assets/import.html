Import Existing FAR

Upload the current Excel or CSV register. The system stages the rows, detects common column names and checks financial integrity before posting.

Up to 100,000 rows per import. Recommended columns: Asset Number, Date Placed in Service, Description, Major Category, Minor Category, Cost, OAD, YTD Depreciation, Accumulated Depreciation and NBV.
Validation rules
  • Duplicate asset numbers and tag numbers are rejected.
  • Cost must be positive; useful life and placed-in-service date are mandatory.
  • Accumulated depreciation cannot exceed cost.
  • NBV must equal Cost less Accumulated Depreciation.
  • Opening accumulated depreciation plus YTD depreciation is checked against accumulated depreciation.
